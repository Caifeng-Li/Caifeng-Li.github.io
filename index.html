01 / ABOUT

Research with a biological perspective.

I am a PhD student in Statistics at the University of Calgary, supervised by Dr. Quan Long and Dr. Jingjing Wu. My work lies at the intersection of statistical genetics, bioinformatics, systems biology, and machine learning.

I am especially interested in when additive models fail to capture complex biology, and how interpretable nonlinear approaches can reveal heterogeneity in disease mechanisms, subtypes, and treatment response.

Before my doctoral studies, I earned an MSc in Computational Biology and Bioinformatics at Beijing Forestry University and gained experience in the medical diagnostics industry.
